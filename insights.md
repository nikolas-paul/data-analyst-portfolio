# Key Insights - Online Retail Dashboard

  ## Executive Summary
  The business shows strong concentration in both geography and product mix.
  A small number of countries and products drive a disproportionate share of
  revenue, while monthly sales patterns indicate seasonality and operational
  planning needs.

  ## Insights
  1. Revenue concentration by country
  A limited set of countries contributes most total revenue. This suggests that
  market-specific pricing, promotions, and logistics improvements in top countries
  can produce outsized impact.

  2. Product concentration (Pareto pattern)
  Top-selling products account for a large share of revenue. Protecting stock
  availability and margin on these products should be prioritized.

  3. Seasonal revenue movement
  Monthly trend lines show visible high and low periods. Forecasting and inventory
  planning should align with these seasonal peaks to avoid stockouts and overstock.

  4. Orders and revenue do not always move together
  Some periods with similar order counts show different revenue levels, indicating
  changes in basket value or product mix.

  5. Data quality has direct KPI impact
  Removing cancellations, negative values, and incomplete customer records
  significantly improves the reliability of reported KPIs.

  ## Recommendations
  1. Focus retention and upsell in top revenue countries first.
  2. Build replenishment alerts for top 10 products.
  3. Plan campaigns 4-8 weeks before historical peak months.
  4. Track both order count and AOV together, not in isolation.
  5. Standardize data validation rules upstream to reduce reporting noise.

  ## Next Iteration

  - Add cohort view by first purchase month
  - Add margin analysis if cost data becomes available
